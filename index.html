Screen setup

Set these on the computer that shows the stimuli. They convert pixel shifts into arcseconds. Saved per browser.

Before testing

  • Browser zoom at 100% (⌘0 / Ctrl+0).
  • Seat the participant 50 cm from the screen, eyes level with its centre.
  • Red lens over the left eye, cyan lens over the right eye.
  • Check: with glasses → shape pops out; one eye closed → shape gone; no glasses → no outline, colour or density cue.
  • Turn brightness up and avoid glare. Use a laptop or tablet, not a phone.
